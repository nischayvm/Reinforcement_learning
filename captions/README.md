## Using these files

Upload the desired lecture Markdown files to your study AI. These are caption transcripts, not summaries. Wording is preserved apart from whitespace, subtitle markup, and repeated rolling captions. Timestamp headings occur approximately every minute and link to the video.

Captions may contain errors in technical terms, equations, or names, including uploaded tracks. Autogenerated tracks are labeled. Visual equations and diagrams are not captured unless spoken in the captions. Raw downloaded tracks and metadata are retained in `raw/`.
